Blue "Crystal" Candy

Description

"Every jib head from here to Timbuktu is going to want a taste." - Jesse(Describing Walter's ultra-pure blue crystal "candy"😏)

This vanilla flavoured sweet sensation is going to make you hold your kids back its so good.

The satisfying cracks and crunches will have you reaching for another, bite after bite.

Ingredients

  • Corn Syrup
  • Granulated Sugar
  • Clear Flavouring Extract
  • Blue Gel Coloring
  • Candy Thermometer

Steps

  1. Combine water, light corn syrup, and granulated sugar in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. Stir until the sugar is moistened and dissolves.
  3. Brush down the sides of the pan with a wet pastry brush – this will remove any stray sugar crystals. Sugar crystals in the pan can cause your whole batch to crystallize.
  4. Once it comes to a boil, insert a candy thermometer. From this point on, do not stir the candy.
  5. Cook the candy until it reaches 290 F on the thermometer. It should be a light yellowish color.
  6. Immediately remove the pan from the heat. Let it sit for a minute, until the rapid bubbling stops, then add flavoring extract and blue gel food coloring.
  7. Stir well until the color is evenly distributed.
  8. Pour the hot candy out onto a baking sheet covered with foil. You can spread it into a thinner layer with the spatula if you’d like.
  9. Let the candy cool completely at room temperature, until it is set and hard.
  10. Use a knife or kitchen malle t to smash it up into smaller pieces.
  11. This candy is easiest to eat in large pieces, so some people prefer to leave it in big chunks. If you want to be more accurate to the show, you can crush it up into smaller chunks.
  12. For optimal awesomeness, I recommend serving this in small baggies at a Breaking Bad viewing party.
